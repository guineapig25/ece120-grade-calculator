How the calculation works

Blank scores are excluded; enter 0 for a missed assignment. Each entered homework, discussion, lab, and assessment is weighted equally within its category, after converting points to percentages. A category with no grades is excluded from the current-grade denominator.

Homework and discussion drop up to two lowest entered grades. Labs drop one lowest eligible entered lab; labs 4, 7, and 8 are protected. At least one entered item is retained per category. Lab code and demo scores are combined by points into one lab grade. If only one portion is entered, its entered points and maximum determine the current lab grade.

Homework 10–14 and labs 5–8 use percentage inputs out of 100. Each quiz is entered as a percentage and adds up to 0.2 percentage points to the course grade (up to 1 total). Extra credit is added after normalizing active course categories.

This is an unofficial calculator using the policy supplied for this tool. Letter grades use strict, unrounded cutoffs; no curve is assumed. A lab average below 50% results in an F regardless of the overall percentage. The lab requirement uses the entered lab grades with the selected lab-drop setting, even if labs are excluded from the overall what-if calculation. Results are provisional until every course score is entered. Scores may exceed the maximum for extra points; negative scores are invalid.
